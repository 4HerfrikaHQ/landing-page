# 4HerFrika

The language of 4HerFrika's mentorship bookings and its Academy courses.

## Language

**Booking**: A scheduled call between one mentor and one mentee.

**Mentee**: The person who booked a call. Not an account; every booking that shares an email address is the same mentee.

**Learner**: Anyone with an Academy account. Open to all, no application. Distinct from a Mentee, who has no account. Avoid "member" for this.

**Partner**: An organisation that co-creates Courses with 4HerFrika (Ordina is the first). Content, not an account.

**Mentor**: A volunteer professional who takes Bookings through Career Corner.

**Enrolment**: One Learner taking one Course. It fixes the Completion policy that Learner is held to.

**Module**: An ordered group of Lessons inside a Course.

**Course**: An ordered set of Modules, each an ordered set of Lessons, co-created with one or more Partners.

**Lesson**: One unit of a Course: a video, slides, text, a Quiz or a Task. Required unless the Course marks it optional.

**Quiz**: A Lesson made of questions, passed at the pass mark its Course's Completion policy sets.

**Task**: A Lesson where the Learner submits their own work, which a Facilitator marks Complete or Needs work. Only exists in Courses whose Completion policy uses Tasks.
_Avoid_: assignment, homework, project

**Facilitator**: A 4HerFrika team member who reviews Tasks for a Course. Not a Mentor, and not a Partner.

**Completion policy**: The rules one Course is completed by, set per Course. 4HerFrika's own Courses use a lean default; a Partner's Course can ask for stricter rules.
_Avoid_: course settings, rules, config

**Course completion**: Every required Lesson complete under the Course's Completion policy.

**Cohort**: The Learners who started a Course in the same calendar quarter. Used only to group Partner reporting; Learners never see it and it gates nothing.
_Avoid_: intake, class, batch

**Certificate**: Proof of one Learner's Course completion, issued once with a public code and verification page. Can be revoked, never edited. Learners and employers see a revoked Certificate as "withdrawn".
